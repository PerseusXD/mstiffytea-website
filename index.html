Airbnb: should you buy at $122?

An investment recommendation for Airbnb stock, built on a free cash flow valuation and a hard look at the risks most models skip.

The question
Airbnb is a great business. Is its stock still worth buying at today's price?
What we did
  • Built a five-year free cash flow (FCFF) model, projecting cash flow from $2.72B in 2025 to $4.96B in 2029, and tested how the fair value moves with the discount rate.
  • Checked the DCF against EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA and P/E multiples and weighted the results.
  • Scored short-term rental regulation risk across NYC, Paris, Spain, Germany and the UK, and assessed upside from the new Instacart partnership.
What came of it
A HOLD call. The DCF showed $136 per share (11.5% upside), but the weighted valuation of $96.20 and regulatory risk meant the price was already fair.

REI: loved brand, lost sales. Then #GearUpAndGo.

A consumer behavior study of REI. I led the research method and results, wrote the case for why people shop there, and created the #GearUpAndGo campaign concept.

The question
REI has strong brand equity. What keeps people from buying there?
What I did
  • Designed a six-section online survey covering shopping behavior, brand awareness, retail experience, perceptions and demographics. Collected 60 responses and flagged the skew toward friends and family.
  • Analyzed the results: brand equity was strong, price was the biggest barrier, and Amazon was the main competitor (75% vs. REI's 35%).
  • Created #GearUpAndGo, which turns REI's once-a-year #OptOutside into a daily push, with nano, micro and macro creators and a membership hook: "Join once for just $30. Adventure for life."

Journi: pitching an AI travel planner, Shark Tank style

Journi builds personalized itineraries in seconds and stays free for travelers. Restaurants and attractions pay $828 a year for placement.

The question
